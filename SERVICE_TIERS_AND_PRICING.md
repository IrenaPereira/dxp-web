# Digital Experiments — Service Architecture & Pricing Model

**Purpose:** Turn a founder-led consulting reputation into a **buyable, productized ladder** an enterprise buyer can scope, expense, and forward internally. This doc has two halves: (1) the internal pricing logic and economics, and (2) exactly how to present it on the site.

---

## 1. The value ladder (land → expand)

Four offers along two axes — **capacity** (how much of us you get) and **altitude** (how strategic the work is). The Audit is the low-risk front door; everything expands from there.

```
ALTITUDE (strategic)
   ▲
   │   ┌─────────────────────────┐
   │   │  ADVISORY               │  exec / leadership, low time
   │   │  Fractional Head of PX  │  high leverage
   │   └─────────────────────────┘
   │   ┌─────────────────────────┐
   │   │  EMBEDDED               │  sustained execution
   │   │  Embedded UX Lead       │  ongoing capacity
   │   └─────────────────────────┘
   │   ┌─────────────────────────┐
   │   │  WORKSHOP               │  team capability
   │   │  Team Alignment Sprint  │  one-time, builds skills
   │   └─────────────────────────┘
   │   ┌─────────────────────────┐
   │   │  AUDIT  ◄── front door  │  fast, fixed-scope, productized
   │   │  Player Experience Audit│  the "easy first yes"
   │   └─────────────────────────┘
   └──────────────────────────────► CAPACITY (hours/week)
```

**The path:** Audit reveals the problems and proves DXP's judgment → Workshop builds the team's skill to address them → Embedded supplies senior hands to execute → Advisory keeps leadership steering. Each tier is a natural upsell of the one below.

## 2. The four offers

### Tier 1 — Player Experience Audit  *(the front door)*
- **For:** A team that suspects UX is costing them players but can't pinpoint where.
- **What it is:** Expert heuristic review + competitive teardown of a flow (onboarding, core loop, store/monetization UX) against player-behavior principles.
- **Deliverable:** Scored findings report, prioritized fix backlog (severity × effort), and a live readout with the team.
- **Two depths:**
  - *Snapshot* — one flow/feature, ~1 week.
  - *Full Audit* — onboarding + core loop + monetization UX, 2–3 weeks.
- **Why it leads:** Fixed scope, fixed fee, fast, expensable without procurement drama. De-risks the relationship and seeds the upsell ("here's what we found — here's how we'd fix it together").

### Tier 2 — Team Alignment Sprint  *(workshop)*
- **For:** A team that has UX problems *and* a skills/alignment gap — UX is one person's job, not the team's practice.
- **What it is:** Facilitated, hands-on workshop(s) that install a shared method and align design/product/eng around the player.
- **Deliverable:** Aligned UX principles, player personas/journey maps built *by the team*, a playtest/usability plan, and a maturity baseline.
- **Two depths:**
  - *Alignment Sprint* — 2-day on-site + synthesis.
  - *UX Foundations Program* — multi-session over 4–6 weeks for a deeper transformation.
- **Why it matters:** This is DXP's stated core strength ("train and align teams"). Productizing it makes it sellable instead of bespoke.

### Tier 3 — Embedded UX Lead  *(embedded)*
- **For:** A team that needs senior UX execution *now* but isn't ready to hire a full-time principal.
- **What it is:** Fractional senior UX leadership embedded with the team — design direction, research cadence, design reviews, mentoring.
- **Deliverable:** Ongoing UX direction and shipped improvements; the team levels up by working alongside a principal.
- **Commitment:** 2–3 days/week, **3-month minimum.**
- **Why it matters:** Recurring revenue for DXP; continuity and capacity for the client.

### Tier 4 — UX Strategy & Advisory  *(consulting)*
- **For:** Studio/publisher **leadership** raising org-wide UX maturity — process, hiring, where UX should report, roadmap.
- **What it is:** A fractional Head of Player Experience at the exec table — low time, high leverage.
- **Deliverable:** UX maturity roadmap, org/process recommendations, exec coaching, hiring support.
- **Commitment:** 2–4 days/month retainer, or day-rate for ad hoc.
- **Why it matters:** Highest altitude, stickiest relationship, positions Irena as a peer to the VP — not a vendor.

## 3. Pricing model (internal)

**Blended rate basis** (2026, premium specialist game-UX consulting):
- **Principal (Irena):** ~$2,500/day — marquee senior specialist, ex-Blizzard/WoW pedigree.
- **Senior associate:** ~$1,200/day — for execution leverage on audits/embeds.

These rates anchor every package below; packages are sold at a fixed/retainer price, not hourly, so DXP captures value (and margin) rather than selling time.

| Offer | Format | Effort (principal-days) | List price | Margin lever |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Audit — Snapshot** | 1 flow, ~1 wk | 4–6 | **$7.5k–$10k** | Productized; reuse heuristic framework |
| **Audit — Full** | core loops, 2–3 wk | 8–12 | **$18k–$25k** | Associate does teardown grunt-work |
| **Workshop — Alignment Sprint** | 2-day on-site + synthesis | 8–12 | **$18k–$25k** | Repeatable curriculum; travel billed separately |
| **Workshop — UX Foundations** | multi-session, 4–6 wk | 15–20 | **$30k–$45k** | Cohort/multi-team pricing |
| **Embedded UX Lead** | 2–3 days/wk, 3-mo min | retainer | **$20k–$32k / month** | Recurring; associate blend lowers cost-to-serve |
| **UX Strategy & Advisory** | 2–4 days/mo | retainer | **$8k–$15k / month** (or **$3k/day** ad hoc) | Pure principal; highest $/day |

**Sanity checks:**
- Embedded @ 2 days/wk ≈ 8.7 days/mo × $2,500 ≈ **$21.6k** → retainer floor $20k. @ 3 days/wk ≈ $32.5k → ceiling $32k. ✔
- Advisory commands the *highest day rate* ($3k) because it's pure principal at exec altitude, even though monthly $ is lower than Embedded (less time). Make this axis explicit so Advisory doesn't read as "the cheap one."

**Land-and-expand lever:** *Credit 100% of the Audit fee toward any Workshop or Embedded engagement booked within 60 days.* Makes the first "yes" nearly free-feeling and pulls buyers up the ladder.

## 4. How to present it publicly

**Recommendation: show three headline cards + one advisory strip.** The buyer asked for "three tiers" — display Audit / Workshop / Embedded as the three cards (the value ladder a team walks), with Advisory as a leadership option beneath. This resolves the four-offers-but-three-tiers tension cleanly.

**Show anchored "starting at" prices — do not hide them.** Enterprise buyers need a number to budget and to forward internally. Hidden pricing reads as "expensive and slow." Anchors + a proposal CTA is the sweet spot.

**Public presentation:**

> **Three ways to work with us**
>
> | **Audit** | **Workshop** | **Embedded** |
> |---|---|---|
> | *Find what's costing you players.* | *Make UX your whole team's job.* | *Senior UX leadership, embedded.* |
> | Expert teardown + prioritized fixes, in 1–3 weeks. | Hands-on training that aligns design, product & eng around the player. | A principal UX lead working inside your team, 2–3 days/week. |
> | **from $7,500** | **from $15,000** | **from $20,000/mo** |
> | [Book an audit →] | [Plan a workshop →] | [Talk to us →] |
>
> **For studio leadership:** *Fractional Head of Player Experience* — strategy, org design, and UX maturity roadmapping at the exec level. **From $8,000/mo.** [Start a conversation →]
>
> *Audit fee credited toward any Workshop or Embedded engagement booked within 60 days.*

**Procurement-friendly add-ons to surface near the pricing:** fixed-fee on Audits/Workshops, NDA-ready, experience with unreleased IP, can work embedded with producers. These remove the enterprise buyer's silent objections.
