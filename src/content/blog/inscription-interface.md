Inscription was an entirely new system for World of Warcraft, and it needed an
interface that felt at home in an established world without confusing veteran
players.

We started from a rudimentary design document and a general concept, then built
toward a fully functional inscription interface. The idea began with a summoning
circle and slowly expanded — glyphs, slots, and glowing geometry — into a system
that maintained the optimal play experience while introducing genuinely new
character customization.
