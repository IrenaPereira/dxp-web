**THE CONTEXT**

** _Blankos Block Party_** is a multiplayer online game developed by Mythical Games. It is a unique combination of a party game, a sandbox game, and a digital toy. In Blankos Block Party, players can customize and control vinyl toy characters called "Blankos" within a virtual world.

The Skills section, located within the game's main menu, provides players with the ability to customize character skills and assign special abilities. This project breakdown focuses primarily on addressing the challenges related to the Skills feature in Blankos Block Party. These challenges include the absence of a clear method to differentiate between skill types, which are divided into **Passive** and **Active** skills, as well as a lack of definition for each of the skills on the interface.

**THE PROBLEM**

How might we improve the Skills screen for an improved sense of navigation, definition and usability?

![Skills Redesign](/blog-images/skills-redesign/Group-88003.png)

We highlighted some UX issues with the current design of the Skills Screen. 

**Passive Skills** include _Foundation_ & _Power_ , whereas the **Active Skills** are the coloured icons beside the character, which do not respond to any interaction from the user whatsoever. 

_Foundation Skills_ are fundamental moves whereas _Power Skills_ are meant to be boosts. Both require assignment and there are 3 slots to fill each. 

**THE GOALS**

**To Improve Understanding of the Feature**

In Blankos Block Party, skills are divided into subsets and are specific to each character, meaning they cannot be swapped or replaced. However, the current presentation of skills in the game may not provide immediate clarity on how they are used during gameplay.

**Fixing Information Architecture**

The information display should be tailored to what is essential for the player's purpose. This involves optimizing the screen space to prioritize Skills, providing their definitions and previews for application, along with basic character information and their corresponding artwork.

**WHAT WE DID**

**Passive Skill 1: Foundation Skill Flow**

![Skills Redesign](/blog-images/skills-redesign/Screen-1-1.png)

This is the landing page the player sees when they enter Skills. Active Skills are placed directly under the Blankos Character. 

On the right side, players can find their Passive Skills - **Foundation** and **Power.**

![Skills Redesign](/blog-images/skills-redesign/1-Foundation-Library-1.png)

To access customisation options for both, players can utilise the console joystick to toggle between the options. Toggling into **Foundation** expands the foundation skill slots. 

![Skills Redesign](/blog-images/skills-redesign/Foundation-Library-Locked-Skill-Selected-1.png)

Hovering over each of the _foundation_ skill pops up a description explaining the skill option and a preview demonstrating it in play.
