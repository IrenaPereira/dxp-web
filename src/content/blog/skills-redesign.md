The Blankos team at Mythical Games had special features to ship over a
"crunch-worthy" timeline. We integrated into the team within 24 hours of first
contact and were turning around working Figma files within days for feedback.

## The problem

> How might we improve navigation of the Skills screen for better speed,
> accessibility and understanding?

## What we did

1. Added a **preview** for skill demonstration and definition.
2. **Unified access** to different types of skill by letting players toggle
   between subtypes for passive skills.
3. Allowed for **character switching** directly in Skills edit mode.

The work redesigned access to the game's skill and inventory features as well
as special event experiences that required easy onboarding for new players.
