Every open-world MMO needs fast travel. The interesting design question isn't
*whether* to include it, but how to shape the time investment around it.

We approached the fast travel map as a way to create larger, logical blocks of
time — reducing tedium while giving players natural moments to step away from
the screen. The result was an interface solution for a genuinely complex
navigation problem.
