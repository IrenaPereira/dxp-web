Project Copernicus was an ambitious open-world MMO. One of our tasks was the
fast travel system — because every open-world MMO needs fast travel.

Our goal was to make the time investment less tedious, and to create larger
logical blocks of time so the system offered players an opportunity to re-enter
the real world for something like food, a bathroom break, or to call their mom.

The game ultimately faced setbacks when the company behind it struggled
financially — but the design thinking behind its navigation systems is still
worth studying.
