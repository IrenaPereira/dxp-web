Chief Puzzle Officer was a game thematically built around corporate espionage
with an anime wackiness — and it presented huge UX challenges. It was a
card-builder *and* featured multiplayer gameplay, both in real time.

Our work brought AAA know-how to an indie mobile title, evolving the battle
screen through several iterations to make a dense, fast, real-time experience
legible and fun on a small screen.
