SciPlay's Jackpot Party is one of the largest social casino titles in the world,
which makes it a fascinating — and high-stakes — UX problem. We conducted lean
user research and retrospectives to interpret the experience by demographic, and
to inform game direction with what players actually wanted from the social
casino experience.
